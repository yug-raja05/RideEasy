Frequently Asked Questions

What do I need to rent a bike or scooter?

You'll need a valid government-issued ID (driver's license or passport) and a credit or debit card for the security deposit. You must be 18 years or older to rent.

What is the cancellation policy?

You can cancel your booking up to 24 hours before your scheduled pickup time for a full refund. Cancellations within 24 hours will incur a 50% cancellation fee.

How much is the security deposit?

The security deposit varies by vehicle type, typically ranging from $50 to $200. This amount is held on your card and fully refunded upon return of the vehicle in good condition.

Do you provide helmets?

Yes! Safety is our priority. All rentals include a free helmet. We also provide locks and basic safety equipment at no extra charge.

Can I extend my rental period?

Absolutely! You can extend your rental by contacting us via phone, chat, or through your account dashboard. Extensions are subject to availability.

What happens if the vehicle is damaged?

Minor wear and tear is expected, but any significant damage will be assessed and charged accordingly. We recommend checking the vehicle before departure and reporting any issues immediately.
